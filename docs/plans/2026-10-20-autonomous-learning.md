# Learn enabling practices from an autonomous project's evolution

## Cold-start summary

The maintainer asks to incorporate useful lessons from the studied autonomous
research and writing project into this template. Negative experience should
also reveal better questions, methods and capabilities, rather than accumulate
prohibitions. The maintainer additionally asks whether the assessment considered
the changes and turning points across hundreds of commits. The preceding study
was selective and recent; it did not establish that longitudinal claim.

This change will reconcile the existing learning and research owners, add
portable examples and bounded evidence, and provide a traceable historical
assessment. It preserves the pre-existing October 19 working-tree changes.
It does not change the studied project or its runtime.

## Complete end state

1. Negative experience helps select an enabling response and tests whether the
   diagnosed failure is solved without losing useful capability.
2. Existing principles remain at their owners. Worked material shows how to use
   them; the change adds no universal checklist, review tier or startup ledger.
3. Historical conclusions distinguish commit metadata, inspected diffs and
   artifacts, project-authored reports, direct probes and unobserved runtime.
4. The final account explains both substantive growth and recurring failures,
   without treating a later version or more commits as proof of improvement.
5. Projections, evidence and plan state are reconciled; required checks pass.
