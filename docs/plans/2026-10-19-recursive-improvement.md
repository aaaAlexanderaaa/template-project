# Make improvement change the next judgment

## Cold-start summary

On 2026-10-19 the maintainer authorized improving this template from the full
research and discussion of an autonomous research and writing project. The
owner's central distinction is judgment quality versus execution effort:
amplifying a mistaken approach can make the system worse. Observability must
also expose mistaken confidence and remain open to correction. The aim is to
attenuate harmful feedback while enabling useful creation and learning.

This is an implementation request, not another source-project assessment.
Baseline: clean `1099ddc6568af5bb2e4a1d080dfc315653cbff58`. Existing rules already
require useful outcomes, active comparisons, positive experience, and method
revision. The gap to test is their effect at decisions: diagnosing judgment
instead of prescribing effort, making observations consequential, letting new
material reshape work, and preserving useful duties through simplification.

Completed: current owners and normal consumers reconciled, frozen baseline and
revised trials observed, independent review and repository checks passed.
The implementation and its bounded evidence are retained in the
[delivery record](../evidence/2026-10-19-recursive-improvement.md).

## Complete end state

1. Recurring correction or successful execution with disappointing value
   triggers investigation of judgment, observation and response before more
   activity is prescribed. Sound ready work can still accelerate directly.
2. Review uses the artifact and scope that can establish its claim; an observed
   problem and the decision made about it remain distinguishable.
3. Consequential method changes compare gains and losses, including useful
   distinctions, exploration and improvement duties, within existing authority.
4. Creative inquiry can change the question and whole artifact, retaining what
   made positive work useful rather than just attaching new material.
5. Normal entry paths retrieve these requirements conditionally. Routine work
   gains no universal classification form, review tier, dashboard or self-doubt
   ritual. Existing permissions and reviewer independence remain binding.

## Execution and verification

1. Record coverage and gaps at current owners; obtain independent read-only
   discovery without giving a desired implementation or verdict.
2. Freeze baseline instructions and a portable case packet before editing.
   Run fresh baseline and revised consumers on that same packet. Preserve their
   initial actions, supplied inputs and actual context limits. The packet must
   include worthwhile creation, decision/effort confusion, review scope, lost
   improvement duties, and a routine burden countercase. It must not require
   private source-project knowledge.
3. Reconcile normative owners before their guides and template consumers.
   Prefer replacement or a precise trigger over repeated general reminders.
4. Inspect actual consumer artifacts and choices, not keyword compliance or
   agreement alone. A baseline success remains a success; these bounded trials
   cannot establish long-term or weaker-executor effectiveness.
5. Obtain independent final review, resolve supported findings, run both required
   Python 3.11+ checks, inspect the public diff, and close this plan with evidence.

Resource bound: one discovery review, two initial consumer trials and one final
review, with at most two focused correction rechecks if a consequential defect
requires them. Each assignment carries its reading scope, output path and stop
condition. No external evaluation calls are planned. Stop comparisons when their
bounded questions are answered; do not repeat until a favorable answer appears.
