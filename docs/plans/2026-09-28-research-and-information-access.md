# Research and information access: first implementation

## Cold-start summary

On 2026-09-28 the maintainer authorized the recommended first implementation
and explicitly asked that it preserve the whole discussion, not only the
latest experience brief. The accepted scope is evidence discovery plus source
access and reuse, using existing normative owners and on-demand methods.

The original problem chain, in English paraphrase, is:

1. Assess discipline through Meadows' leverage points, including the effects
   of changing recurring defaults rather than automatically adding rules.
2. Grill should find consequential problems against the owner's supported
   purpose and standards. Public method and privately supplied taste stay
   separate; existing onboarding should be reused.
3. Research too easily produces a story around a few discoverable articles.
   Explore independent observations, perspectives, contradictions, entities,
   references, and evolution; verify the decisive claim rather than citation
   formatting or existence alone.
4. Costly access exploration should produce usable experience for the next
   task instead of being repeated from scratch.
5. Capability, context loading, and separate agents are different choices.
   Ordinary agents still need information; every task cannot load all methods.
6. The supplied methodology and first-party brief are fallible references.
   They refine the design but do not replace the preceding aims or authorize
   all their suggested mechanisms.

The [assessment](../evidence/2026-09-28-research-and-information-acquisition.md)
preserves candidate wording, public research findings and limits, and source
triage. Its approval state is a historical snapshot; this plan records the
subsequent explicit implementation authorization.

## Risk classification

Material reusable guidance: changes inquiry behavior and its public reading
routes. No authorization, isolation, scheduler, product runtime, or account
configuration is changed. High-risk design review is not activated. Fresh
consumer use is required by the development contract's user-facing delivery
boundary. Rule effectiveness across adopters will remain unproved.

## Complete end state

- Existing inquiry and fallback sections own the added rules and their limits.
- Two independently callable guides explain research and information access;
  either can be used by the current agent or an appropriately bounded worker.
- Root and operation routes select methods without loading the whole library.
- Research output distinguishes observations, interpretation, unresolved
  alternatives and final claims; success need not produce a new narrative.
- Access work checks what a method can observe, retrieves applicable prior
  experience before retries, and returns reusable knowledge to a findable owner.
- Ordinary lookup remains short. No platform, citation, reviewer or query
  count becomes a quality threshold; private taste remains optional context.

## Execution and verification

1. Update the two existing contract sections and their dated source anchor.
2. Add guides and compact routes; reconcile projections without copying rules.
3. Exercise fresh consumer use, source-access reuse, and countercases. Correct
   consequential findings at their owner and recheck affected behavior.
4. Run required Python checks, inspect actual diff and public boundaries,
   update evidence and this plan, and close the supported implementation layer.

Verification budget: two fresh read-only consumer assignments, at most six web
calls each, with additional correction review only for an actual finding.
One uses research instructions on a bounded public-source question; the other
uses access instructions and a permitted method record in fresh context.
Each also exercises a normal or failure countercase. Stop on the assigned
result, exhausted budget, or unavailable capability; record missing evidence.
No paid external service or new credentials are introduced. Actual call counts
and available cost observations will be reported; unreported token spend stays
unknown. These trials demonstrate use, not controlled comparative efficacy.
