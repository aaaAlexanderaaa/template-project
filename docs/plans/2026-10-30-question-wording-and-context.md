# Preserve question wording and review conditions

## Requested outcome and authority

The owner corrected the checklist design: an AI can produce different judgments
about the same artifact when wording, supplied context and its relationship to
the work change. They supplied three distinct article questions: an unfamiliar
reviewer receiving negative feedback, the contextual author receiving negative
feedback, and the contextual author asked to assess readability. They report
decreasing diagnostic effectiveness in that order and want the assistant,
project maintainers, users and question-asking AIs to understand the difference.

This change makes that correction usable through the existing methods and
evidence surfaces. The broader project knowledge-catalog design remains a
proposal. The owner authorizes ChatGPT collaboration for this work; private
history and credentials are outside the handoff.

Baseline: clean `3089af1af089aacb4862c9d1dd4ac2991d289fa0`.

the review CheatSheet already contains context-isolation and framing advice.

generic self-check. Existing knowledge did not change the selected method.

its pending historical trials are not claimed or closed by this work.

discussion and consumer evidence, with configured personalization and unknown
model/context details disclosed rather than called an empty environment.

Risk: material reusable guidance, without a high-risk enforcement change.
Use a proportionate plan and consumer trial. Current authorization, independent
review requirements and optional-method selection remain at their owners.
The change adds no launcher, external publication, schedule or required trio
of reviews. No product concern is activated by these documentation changes.

## Complete end state

- The development owner explains why AI question wording and actual review
  conditions belong to the evidence and the method being reused.
- The review guide preserves all three owner-supplied question forms in
  labeled English translation, with distinct recipients and uses.
- General task, question and Grill entrypoints route people and agents to
  that method without copying it into startup instructions.
- Review and evaluation records make self-check, contextual critique and
  independent consumer observations distinguishable.
- A consumer can select the method and prepare a dispatch on a bounded case, identify
  unverifiable isolation, and preserve ordinary low-cost work.
- Evidence distinguishes reported experience, external research, discussion,
  instruction use and an unperformed causal ranking experiment.

## Execution and verification

For this correction, budget two ChatGPT calls: one design discussion and one
instruction-use consumer. Add a follow-up only for a concrete unresolved result;
stop when the bounded use cases and review findings are settled. Earlier calls
in the parent design discussion remain separate historical work.

1. Reconcile development's existing analysis owner before dependent guidance.
2. Update the review method, entrypoint links and evidence prompts in place.
3. Give a separate ChatGPT consumer the delivered guidance and case material,
   without the author's rationale or desired answers. Preserve its first report
   and correct supported findings. This is a bounded instruction-use trial,
   not a demonstration that one prompt is universally better.
4. Run the required Python unit suite, documentation checker and whitespace
   check. Verify normal entrypoints and the complete-rule delivery route.
5. Record results and limitations, reconcile affected projections, and close
   this plan only for the delivered method and its observed use.
