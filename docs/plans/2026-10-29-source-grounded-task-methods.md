# Source-grounded task methods

## Requested outcome and authority

The owner accepted the recommendations in the
[public-method study](../evidence/2026-10-28-public-task-method-research.md),
then asked to mine their historical conversations in a designated local project:
some questions can become useful task entrypoints directly, with light editing
or explicit combination. This extends the general practical layer delivered
on October 28. Review remains one family among many; visualization is not the
deliverable.

The complete end is a usable, navigable set of concrete task moves, including
recognizable owner-derived questions and the researched improvements to
diagnosis, design comparison and experience reuse. Readers can distinguish
source extraction, translation, synthesis and implementer additions. Evidence
supports the claimed scope and conditions without presenting a prompt catalog
as proven superior practice.

Authority remains with development's
[experience rule](../contracts/development-discipline.md#experience-changes-the-next-matching-action),
[decision attribution](../contracts/development-discipline.md#preserve-decisions-and-evidence)
and [delivery verification](../contracts/development-discipline.md#verify-promised-and-observed-behavior-in-both-directions).
The [analysis contract](../contracts/data-analysis-discipline.md) governs this
qualitative source study. Existing authorization permits these reversible
repository edits and their required verification. The neighboring checkout is
read-only source material; its instructions, schedules and roles are not
adopted here. Personal transcripts stay local.

## Execution and ownership

Root owns source selection, guide integration and final reconciliation. It may
write the existing general guide, a focused question collection if useful,
their normal navigation, this plan and bounded evidence. No product, harness
behavior, external publication, neighboring file or unrelated work changes.

Independent work completed read-only except for its dedicated evidence:
`history_source_review` owns `2026-10-29-task-method-source-review.json`;
`task_entry_consumer` owns `2026-10-29-task-method-consumer.json`, both under
`docs/evidence/`. Neither edited guidance or saw the other's observations.
Root integrates their completed records and owns final publication checks.

1. Inspect the original-message snapshot and extraction method. Keep a
   reproducible selection ledger with source identity, context, transformation
   and exclusion reasons. Feedback summaries are locators, not verbatim proof.
2. Select task-changing interventions across real scenarios. Preserve effective
   question forms rather than translating everything into another meta-rule.
   Improve the diagnosis, comparison and retrospective entries in place.
3. Put concise questions at a normal task doorway. Keep public provenance in
   repository evidence and private source locators in the session-local ledger.
   Explain enough for a consumer who lacks this conversation and source checkout.
4. Obtain separate independent source/claim assessment and fresh-context use
   of the public guidance. Reviewers first give their unhinted judgments, then
   root investigates findings and verifies corrections. Workers receive only
   their bounded task and real consumer inputs; no external service receives
   private history. Record actual context isolation and evidence limits.
5. Run the template's required unit suite, documentation checker and whitespace
   checks. Reconcile the guides, evidence and plan with the observed result.

## Verification and risks

| Claim or risk | Evidence and response |
|---|---|
| A question really comes from the owner | Original snapshot record, date/line and text identity; mark translation/edit/merge explicitly; disclose missing original session evidence |
| Short assignments convey the distinctive move | Independent consumer selects and uses an entry on a task outside review; retain its first interpretation and any required rescue |
| Historical settings become universal mandates | Keep purpose and invariants, parameterize quantities; exclude schedules, model choices, project-specific standing bans and consensus-as-truth |
| More prose hides the useful move | Keep copyable prompts short, route details to existing owners, and inspect ordinary use plus a simple countercase |
| A trial is mistaken for comparative effectiveness | Report observed use and failure boundaries; do not claim numerical gains or causal superiority from a single unpaired trial |
| Existing work is overwritten | Preserve the initial dirty tree; review this change's files and do not commit or reset unrelated changes |
