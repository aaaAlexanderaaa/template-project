# Experience that changes subsequent work

## Cold-start summary

The maintainer authorized adopting the integrated lessons from the
`between-ideas` study after correcting several partial interpretations. The
source experiment sustains autonomous research and content production; its
growth, successful adaptations, repeated mistakes, and owner interventions
are the learning material. The accepted outcome connects creative inquiry,
mission and task continuity, experience reuse, self-assessment, acceleration,
and adaptation under growing complexity. It is not another feedback catalog.

Baseline: `560696d2fc80d06474010f32e413ecbfefcef6de`, clean working tree.
The source study inspected `between-ideas` at
`c6a7c562b04fb6ee9a4f5bb084c86b6e82a13d1c`. Private source transcripts stay
outside this repository. The final owner instruction on 2026-10-10 explicitly
authorizes implementation of the integrated proposal.

The October 11 refinement adds active discriminating comparisons, source and
model dependence accounting, and mechanism-based scale activation. Both initial
integration and the authorized refinement are complete in the working tree.
Fresh consumers using two models applied the final drafts to seven synthetic
cases; their first results and limits are retained in the existing evidence
record. This establishes bounded rule consumption, not longitudinal improvement.

## Authority, scope, and risk

Material documentation change: reconcile existing normative owners, their
entry paths, guides, and templates, and align the suite's fixed clock with the
reviewed repository fixture date. The handoff inventory replaces its pitfalls
heading with reusable experience, preserving both successes and failures.
No product runtime, scheduler, live charter,
source experiment, or personal-memory package is changed. Architecture remains
the unconfigured engineering-discipline template. No new authority or risk
acceptance is delegated. This route requires artifact and consistency review;
it does not activate high-risk independent review. Initial same-context review
and the later fresh consumer comparisons are labeled separately.

The main risks are more administration without changed defaults, experience
turning into universal prohibitions, self-approval disguised as autonomy,
invalid cached verification, and model intelligence being credited to the
template. Current owners must govern both the useful action and its limit.

## Authorized refinement — 2026-10-11

The owner requests active experiments that create useful differences in
evidence, material organization, questions or stance, and model selection,
rather than relying only on critical attitude or repeated same-model review.
The owner also accepts the proposed conditional scale trigger and smallest
discriminating scenario. The October 10 handoff correction is retained.
This reopens the same integration effort; its initial closure above remains
historical evidence, not a verdict on the refinement.

Material route: development owns the active comparison duty; agent execution
owns independence evidence and scale activation. Reconcile their research,
Grill, operating, and template consumers without changing charter authority or
required reviewer separation. Preserve the original uncommitted work.

1. Update the current owners with experiment triggers, useful variation,
   genuine source independence, model variation, stopping and evidence limits.
2. Connect normal inquiry and review routes and templates; make the scale
   projection conditional and retain accumulation analysis before failures occur.
3. Exercise fresh read-only consumers on bounded synthetic cases, including
   stable repeated work, hidden accumulation, shared evidence, unavailable

   supplied material; do not call split material independent acquisition.
4. Correct supported findings within the authorized refinement, run the Python 3.11+ suite and documentation
   checker, record results and limits, and close this refinement.

Coordination: the parent is the sole editor and integration owner. Any consumer
probe is read-only, receives the owner outcome and current governing sections,
and stops after its bounded cases and findings. Drafts are stable while probes
read them; changes are announced here before re-verification. Two available
models may be compared within this local validation, with no external writes.
Bound: two initial consumer probes and at most two correction rechecks, each
limited to the supplied cases and supported findings. Record unavailable
capabilities and actual dispatch rather than implying a model trial occurred.

existing tolerance; date validation is not relaxed.
