## Rule classes and complete delivery

[Task context](../README.md#task-context) distinguishes four reading classes:
global required core, activated mandatory detail, selected execution method,
and provenance/history. Authority, applicability, required text, rule strength
and completion evidence answer separate questions. Mandatory detail adds to
core; it is not replaced by a richer summary. Unchanged, retained units can be
reused; restart and handoff recover current source through its locators.

The actual core preparation supplies eight units and 88,704 source bytes.
Development core includes original-purpose selection and decision attribution;
governance core includes engineering discretion and human-owned decisions;
execution supplies risk, smallest route and layered completion. This is a
substantial cold-start reading cost. Neither a shorter lookup nor a passing
manifest demonstrates that this cost is optimal or that an agent understood it.

The producing context obtained complete core bodies, using digest-checked parts
when output was truncated; changed sources were prepared again. The independent
consumer also encountered truncation and recovered the missing text by parts.
Recovery guards preserve exact UTF-8 bytes, complete section boundaries and
source identity. A completed transcript establishes source delivery. Procedure
use and result evidence remain separate obligations.

## Limits and completion judgment

The observations support a usable template entry path, explicit reading
classes, complete declared detail, a practical SOP and outcome-based stopping
cases. Source/procedure exercises and fresh-consumer judgment do not establish
universal compliance, low cold-start cost, measured time savings or external
project adoption. Automatic declaration checks cannot infer every applicable
concern or replace the operator's semantic judgment.

This earlier bounded exercise supported entrypoint/procedure improvement and
closed its particular non-graphical routing finding. The subsequent review
reopened template acceptance: global profile definitions and service detail
are missing from the declared route, and normal product templates conflict
with the reader's role validation. The SOP and original-outcome distinctions
remain useful. Complete-template acceptance is partial; the linked later review
owns the new observations, external transfer trial and factual correction.
