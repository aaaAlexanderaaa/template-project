# Reading repairs and practical task methods

## Authorized outcome

The owner requested implementation of the independent review findings and
practical common-task methods. They identified the same-task reviews
and external consumer as a useful pattern, and asked to learn from less
systematized review methods in `between-ideas`. The reusable layer is a
CheatSheet beneath the task SOP: selected moves and copyable assignments that
help an actual decision. It adds no standing agent schedule or universal quota.
The executor initially narrowed this to review. The owner clarified that
review was an example of concise, effective task methods across the template's
scenarios. The reading repairs and review cards retain their evidence; broader
method delivery and representative non-review verification now follow that
corrected interpretation.

The [implementation plan](../plans/2026-10-28-rule-reading-implementation.md)
retains the original template-improvement outcome and this accepted extension.

## Source selection and attribution

The [source record](2026-10-28-review-method-sources.json) identifies selected
files, digests and actual reading scope. Inspection was local and read-only.
No neighboring runtime, scheduled task or review launcher was run. Personal
quotations, account settings, schedules and source-project mandates remain at
their source. The guide's questions are implementer adaptations authorized by
the current request, not verbatim statements attributed to that owner.

The neighboring records report several useful distinctions:

- A neutral comprehension question, a deliberately critical question and a
  collection comparison found different problems in the same material.
- A single item could be understandable while the collection repeated an
  imposed formula; these results answer different questions.
- The project's report of a fresh subagent was corrected after discovering
  injected project instructions. Its later launcher records context isolation
  as unverified rather than inferring it from a temporary directory.
- Repeated questions tested stability, while varied questions opened other
  answer directions. A unique useful finding retained its value without a vote.
- An attribution audit followed claimed authority back to source, and later
  inspection identified findings that were repeatedly rediscovered without
  correction. Finding production and actual repair have different evidence.

These are reported project observations, not a newly controlled experiment or
a universal effectiveness ranking. The source's fixed counts, always-new
question requirement and append-only question bank were not promoted into
this template's mandatory rules.

## Reusable delivery

[The practical task CheatSheet](../guides/task-cheatsheet.md) is the general
entrypoint. It routes from an actual obstacle to a concrete action, usable
result and sufficient stop. Twelve copyable prompts cover project understanding,
intent clarification, diagnosis, research, access recovery, comparison,
complete delivery, explanation, session recovery, experience reuse,
simplification and completion judgment. Specialized review and bounded
extraction remain linked choices. These are representative recipes and a way
to construct others, rather than a closed catalog of the template's tasks.

The broad methods are adaptations of current development, governance and
execution owners and their existing guides. The neighboring-project evidence
below supports the review specialization, not a claim that all task families
were tested there. Recipes can be combined or changed for the decision at
hand. The SOP still orders execution; contracts retain duties and authority.
No new required template, automatic worker or universal checklist is introduced.

[The review CheatSheet](../guides/review-cheatsheet.md) contains common dispatch
templates for two same-task independent reviewers plus a separate consumer,
artifact-only comprehension, and generating a new review question. It also
offers doorway reading, wrong-summary rehearsal, collection comparison,
self-application, normal-copy trials, premise removal and attention-cost probes.

The guide helps choose a move for an uncertainty and reconciles answers into
an evidence-backed action or stop. Existing owners retain authority,
independence, privacy and required review boundaries. It is reachable through
README, Grill, operation, the guide index and review/template entrypoints.
The [one-practice card](../guides/project-operation.md#take-one-practice-into-a-task)
supports bounded extraction without claiming a full survey or exempting governed
work from complete activated detail.

The general doorway is reachable from README, task context, the operation SOP,
guide index and template index. Current preparation still delivers nine core
units, with the optional method file outside the mandatory reading closure.
The full method or activated owner is obtained when the task selects it.

## Observations and closure

Failure-first actual-template tests reproduced five role-validation errors.
After the default repair, all 44 focused reader tests pass, including complete
risk definitions, backend/cross-stack dependencies, explicit role rejection and
frontend relocation. These establish local source delivery and normal template
consumption, not user judgment or long-term adoption.

The [repair observation record](2026-10-28-template-repair-observations.json)
retains the two independent first reports, actual-template probe, consumer's
first understanding and eight process observations, source digests and recheck
decisions. Both reviewers received identical open assignments in fresh
contexts. They confirmed the normal template paths and static/interactive
scope distinction, while independently finding the broad consumer trigger.
Root corrected that existing owner after all first reports returned. Both
rechecked and closed the finding; only development changed during that recheck.

The normal preparation now supplies nine units, including formal risk
definitions. Actual generic/backend/cross-stack adoption probes emit 10/11/12
units; the filled canonical fixture also passes the checker. This establishes
metadata, link, lifecycle and declared-reading compatibility, not semantic
adoption by a deployed product. The static PNG and interactive sibling were
contract exercises, not newly built visual products.

A separate consumer received only the CheatSheet and extraction-card data
copies, totaling 11,487 bytes, with no expected takeaway list. It selected a
normal-use/countercase card, made a bounded fictional CLI trial and observed
eight real process outcomes. It found an unstated lexical-width boundary and
kept the owner's intended leading-zero policy unknown rather than inventing it.
Its first understanding, practical assignment and stop decision support the
cards' instructional use. The fictional CLI was authored and reviewed in that
same context; it is not a second independently dispatched review. Known native
project/global instructions remained present, so zero context is not claimed.
The earlier 97 KB extraction task and this smaller packet test different
tasks; their size difference does not establish causal savings.

Prior repair integration: [169 tests](2026-10-28-template-repair-tests.txt) pass in
39.393 seconds; the documentation checker passes with 59 canonical documents
and 16 templates; whitespace checks pass. The source preparation and complete
recovery guards remain covered. The independent-adoption-review promise keeps
its existing owner and deadline.

The bounded reading repair and review-card slice are complete. The known
reading paths work, output-form and routine-review boundaries are explicit,
and a fresh consumer can select and use a review card to produce a supported
result. Those observations alone left the broader non-review request open.

## Non-review consumer and current closure

A newly dispatched consumer entered through README without prior reports,
executor discussion, expected findings or a prescribed method list. The parent
supplied a disposable CLI task with explicit current behavior and a previous
handoff containing an unaccepted interpretation. The consumer saved its first
understanding before execution, selected and combined resume, failing-boundary
and outcome-judgment methods, and completed the authorized correction.

The baseline lost repeated names through set conversion. Distinct names still
sorted correctly; repeated names, a repeated-space sibling and recovery with
repeated input failed. Removing the set conversion produced ten passing
focused process observations. They cover repetitions, case-sensitive order,
spaces, UTF-8, empty lines, error channels, corrected-input recovery and
independent invocations. Per-process directory snapshots show unchanged
persisted contents inside the disposable directory. Source inspection supports
the bounded absence of other effects; no network or transient-write tracing
is claimed. Root inspected the reports, exact correction and input identities.

The consumer then constructed a different task from an actual obstacle: which
source identity protects rule recovery, and what follows after metadata changes?
Following the normal reader locator into implementation established a
whole-file digest comparison and a fresh-prepare instruction. This was a
source-backed maintainer answer, not a new metadata-edit behavioral test.
The separate task shows one concrete instance of constructing a useful task
from this layer rather than merely executing a supplied prompt.

The consumer's supported judgment was a pass for these two tasks, with no
consequential usability defect exposed. The reader correctly rejected an
external README binding; direct complete-source reading under the trial's
explicit owner authority preserved the task without copying governance.
Governed startup delivered 97,685 bytes including its manifest, substantially
more than this tiny correction. The recipe did not introduce that core cost,
and this work establishes no startup-efficiency improvement.

The [observation record](2026-10-28-template-repair-observations.json) preserves
first understanding, actual before/after observations, reproduction sources,
source-question evidence, first evaluation and parent reconciliation under
`practical_methods`. It also records the trial's retirement. Known native
instructions remained present; this is a fresh consumer context, not verified
zero context, independent evidence acquisition or a model comparison.

Current integration passes all 169 tests in 40.049 seconds, the checker with
60 canonical documents and 16 templates, and whitespace verification. The
general method file stays outside the nine-unit required core. The bounded
template repair and practical-method delivery are complete: there is a normal
entrypoint, usable methods beyond review, a way to construct others, and actual
consumer work supporting that capability. The independent-adoption-review
promise remains open. Effectiveness of every recipe, benefits over an already
capable agent, future independent reuse and sustained external adoption remain
unproved.
