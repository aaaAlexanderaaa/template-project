# Questions from practice and researched task moves

## Outcome and decision attribution

The owner accepted the [public-method recommendations](2026-10-28-public-task-method-research.md)
and added that questions in their historical conversations could be used almost
directly, lightly edited or combined. This delivery improves the existing
[general CheatSheet](../guides/task-cheatsheet.md) and supplies
[question starters](../guides/task-starters.md) across project direction,
architecture, explanation, research, comparison, repeated work and repair.
The implementer chose the selection, English wording and navigation. The owner
did not author those translations or independently endorse every resulting
prompt as effective.

The [delivery plan](../plans/2026-10-29-source-grounded-task-methods.md) records
the authorized outcome. Review is a related specialization, not the boundary
of this work. No visualization or product interface was built.

## Coverage and method

The local source was a project-filtered user-message snapshot generated on
October 23. It contains 580 timestamped entries from September 14 through
October 23. The implementer read every entry in chronological order and
recovered the one truncated message from its companion full-text artifact.
The [sanitized inventory](2026-10-29-task-method-sources.json) records the
snapshot digest, record ordinals, body digests and timestamp limitations.
Private paths, session identifiers and original transcripts stay in a
session-local provenance ledger rather than this public template.

The source's history-access method and extractor were inspected as data.
They filter project directory, user role, origin, session type, synthetic
markers, known dispatch text and scheduled-task matches; they also deduplicate
by the first 120 characters and redact credential-shaped strings. These are
the inspected extraction rules, not an independent audit of the underlying
live and archived databases. A `real_user` label alone cannot distinguish
human typing from a replay. Historical typing provenance and material after
this snapshot remain unverified. A selected record proves its wording occurs
in this identified artifact, not every claim it makes about the project.

Selection looked for an intervention another task can use: a new observation,
a changed premise, a concrete comparison or a clearer result. Frequency and
negative sentiment were not scores. Positive and constructive material was
considered alongside complaints. The selection retains distinctive questions
instead of converting every incident into a prohibition. Neighbouring messages
and explicit corrections were used to check scope. Feedback-register summaries
and a small quote CSV served as provenance checks, not substitutes for the
actual message. The CSV itself sometimes shortens or changes source wording.

This is a qualitative extraction, with record-level evidence and stated
coverage. The 580 count is the snapshot population, not an effectiveness
sample or an estimate of how often a failure occurs. Assistant responses,
resulting work and later outcomes were not paired for all those messages.
The study therefore establishes useful candidates and attributable question
forms, not a measured ranking of successful prompts.

messages for local verification. In the guide, **translated excerpt** selects
and translates a passage; **edited translation** also parameterizes or adjusts
its wording; **adaptation** reconstructs the move; **synthesis** combines named
records. All use notes outside the copied prompts are implementer guidance.

The operational-case synthesis selects one intervention from H07/H08. The
original asks additional questions about communication cost, concurrent-task
priority, context conflicts and proactive improvement. Those remain useful
directions when relevant; this short case probe does not claim to preserve a
complete workflow-design method.

Supporting records also kept target boundaries visible. H18 distinguishes
detector symptoms from the problem they reveal. H19 asks about useful progress,
attention and elapsed-result cost; its project-specific token budget is not
imported. H21 challenges a reviewer assignment that predetermines its answer.
These informed the selection and trials rather than becoming extra universal
rules.

## Important exclusions and alternatives

The study did not copy every forceful instruction. Examples of exclusions:

- A September 14 request for workers to align until agreement is a local
  instruction. It cannot become consensus as truth or an endless retry rule.
- Source-specific models, hourly schedules, fixed worker counts, deployment
  permissions, topic exclusions and unrestricted continuing mandates do not
  transfer with a question.
- The September 28 long message explicitly contains discussion with another
  AI. Its pasted research recommendations and numerical claims were not
  attributed to the owner or newly validated as research here.
- The October 11 pasted external review identifies itself as external opinion.
  A user-message container does not make its prose the owner's original words.
- Changes from the requested human-reading test to invented personas were
  explicitly corrected in the source. The new collection does not claim a
  fabricated persona is a real reader, or a native worker is blank by default.
- Several October 20 quote-CSV records contain shortened or slightly altered
  wording despite a verified label. The timestamped source text takes priority
  for this extraction; that label was not accepted as verbatim proof.

A wholesale replacement with a public skill framework would give more packaged
procedures, while importing authority, tooling and workflow assumptions this
project already owns. A new field for every complaint would retain more text,
while weakening the short task entrypoint. The selected approach combines
source-shaped questions with concrete techniques at the existing task owners.

## Public techniques integrated

The [prior study and source inventory](2026-10-28-public-task-method-research.md#sources-and-evidence-strength)
retain actual reading scope and rolling-source limits. October 29 changes use:

| Existing entry | Concrete change | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Failure diagnosis | Keep a symptom-sensitive observation failing while minimizing; trace the wrong value upstream; rerun the original case | Pocock diagnosis procedure and Superpowers tracing example |
| Design comparison | Hold purpose and binding constraints common; vary a meaningful design objective; compare the same caller and transferred responsibilities | Design It Twice |
| Experience reuse | Inspect the real operation, locate the responsible default/interface/access path, then observe its next use | Session-based retro |
| Clarification | A worked prerequisite choice and an independent choice in the same frontier | Shared grilling, through the existing SOP |
| Delivery | A usable behavior across necessary layers, with a mechanical-migration exception | Behavioral ticket slicing |
| Understanding | Predict a concrete effect and compare observed behavior with intended behavior | Code-comprehension quiz |
| Simplification | Trace duties that return to callers when an abstraction is removed | Module-removal probe |

These are implementer adaptations of inspected public procedures. No fixed
question quota, worker quota, broad approval ceremony, context-size threshold,
unverified gain statistic or automatic external tooling was adopted. The three
main changes are mechanisms another task can try; their online availability
does not by itself establish that they outperform an experienced agent.

## Independent source assessment

The [source review](2026-10-29-task-method-source-review.json) used a separate
native worker without the producing conversation. It received the accepted
task scope, current repository instructions and the selected private source
ledger, with the broader snapshot available. It verified all 21 selected
body/context identities and assessed all thirteen prompts. Its first verdict
supports their source basis and preserves three observations:

- The initial public plan exposed the neighboring source locator. Root removed
  it; the reviewer rechecked the correction.
- The plan's instruction to put private locators in evidence was ambiguous.
  It now names the session-local ledger, separately from public evidence.
- The H07/H08 case trace selects part of a broader workflow inquiry. Its use
  note and this record now make that scope clear.

The reviewer did not independently reselect from every snapshot body or audit
the original message databases. It inspected the same source, not independently
acquired evidence. Exact model identity and model variation were not established.
The report retains first findings and subsequent rechecks separately.

## Fresh-context use through the normal entrypoint

The [consumer record](2026-10-29-task-method-consumer.json) preserves a separate
worker's first interpretation, actual path, inputs, command observations and
limits. It inherited repository instructions, runtime instructions and a bounded
scaling diagnosis task. It received no producing conversation, private history,
source-selection report or expected recipe choice. It followed README, task
context, the task SOP and the CheatSheet to the growth question without rescue.

The use note asking for one actual addition led to a disposable copy of six
public reader inputs and a real CLI trial. Adding a rule in an inactive domain
left all nine ordinary unit bodies unchanged, at 92,501 bytes. Selecting Time
and calendar added its 4,539-byte unit; a 298-byte addition grew that selected
closure by exactly 298 bytes. A backend-only edit changed the shared file
digest and rejected old core recovery despite identical core text. This
supports conditional reading as a scaling mechanism and exposes its whole-file
transaction trade-off. It does not establish that unchanged retained units
must be reread or that the library now needs restructuring.

The resulting recommendation is useful and bounded: preserve distinct rule
owners and necessary dependency edges, then check inactive and selected reading
on the next real domain addition. No hypothetical rule was adopted in the
repository. Temporary copies were removed after inputs and observations were
retained in the record. Root normalized host-specific command roots in the
public JSON, preserving the exact original record session-locally and declaring
that publication transformation.

The consumer also found that the question collection was offered only when
the current framing was already producing a weak answer. Root changed the
doorway to direction, growth or value, making first-use relevance explicit.
The consumer rechecked the wording and destination. That is a narrow correction
check, not a new unhinted search or a measured findability improvement.

The countercase found the existing frontend surface template directly and
required no plan or review trial. The SOP already teaches that exact lookup,
so it proves this route stays simple, rather than general unseen-task routing.

## Verification and supported end

The evidence supports attributable question forms, a portable public collection,
and one useful independent task use. The original recipe authorship remains
unchanged; the researched additions are labeled adaptations. Other starters and
the refined diagnosis, design-comparison and retrospective recipes were not
individually trialed in this round. No controlled effectiveness comparison,
long-running reuse or numerical improvement is claimed.

Repository validation is separate:

- Python 3.14.2: `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_*.py'` passed
  all 169 tests in 41.040 seconds.
- Source checks matched the snapshot and all selected identities; the public
  question guide, source record, plan and reports were checked for source-path
  disclosure. Source-specific paths remain session-local.
- The default rule-reader closure remains nine complete units. Optional
  question starters add no mandatory reading dependency.
- `python3 scripts/check_docs.py` passed: 64 canonical documents and 16
  templates under the full profile. The existing overdue
  `independent-adoption-review` promise remains an advisory; this task neither
  completed nor rescheduled that separate review.
- `git diff --check`, untracked-text whitespace checks, unique-key JSON parsing
  and reviewed guide/source identity comparisons passed.

This delivery supplies the accepted practical improvements and source-shaped
task entrypoints. Later real use can test which questions improve outcomes or
reduce owner correction.
