## Research findings that constrain the design

| Public source | Supported observation | Design limit |
|---|---|---|
| [STORM](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14207v2), table 5 | With equal generated question counts, the full method found 99.83 unique references versus 54.36 and 39.56 in two ablations | Unique references are not independent observations; equal question counts do not establish equal cost or proportional reliability gains |
| [InForage](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09316), reward design and ablations | Intermediate coverage reward contributed to performance in the studied tasks | The reward uses a supplied expected knowledge set; open research cannot assume that set is known or replace it with a self-awarded score |
| [Failing to Falsify](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02485), tables 2 and 15 | Opposite-testing prompts helped thinking models in a number-rule discovery task; Dual-Goal had mixed results and a negative average effect for the non-thinking group | The reported roughly 42% to 56% improvement does not establish a universal web-research intervention |
| [ACH experiment](https://sjdm.org/~baron/journal/20/200430/jdm200430.html) | A randomized study found no ACH advantage on the tested probability-coherence measures | Institutional use is not proof of effectiveness; this result is also narrower than proving ACH useless |
| [Statistical stopping study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7700715/) | Recall-oriented stopping can be evaluated within a retrieved document pool under stated sampling assumptions | It cannot certify that the open web or an unknown evidence space has been exhaustively searched |

failure mechanisms, and current repository boundaries. No study above tests
the complete proposed template change.

## What the execution brief changes in this assessment

The important addition is that information can be distorted while it is
observed or evaluated, even when a tool returns successfully or reviewers agree.
Discovery, source access, observation validity, and owner judgment therefore
need distinguishable responsibilities. They need not become four agents.

Two consequences deserve particular care:

- A standards reviewer needs relevant, authorized goal and preference evidence.
  A fresh consumer needs what the real consumer would receive. Giving both the
  owner's complete background can hide an explanation that fails on its own.
  The existing [Grill procedure](../guides/grill.md#preconditions) and
  [consumer verification](../contracts/development-discipline.md#verify-promised-and-observed-behavior-in-both-directions)
  already provide these different routes.
- A simulated person's confusion is a hypothesis about people, just as their
  satisfaction is. A directly observed broken link or missing prerequisite can
  establish an artifact defect; an invented persona's reported reaction cannot
  establish audience prevalence, demand, comprehension, or benefit. Merely
  changing positive stories into negative stories does not repair the method.

Repeated agreement followed by owner rejection calls for examining the shared
question, inputs, evidence, and capability. More identical reviewers can repeat
the same omission. Existing [review closure](../contracts/autonomous-operation-discipline.md#reviewer-findings-are-answered-and-independently-closed)
already names this response. Human feedback calibrates preference; factual
claims still need appropriate observations. Neither side substitutes for the
other.

## Proposed first change, ready for a scoped decision

The proposed scope is two conditional methods, using existing normative owners.
The paragraphs below are candidate behavior, not presently adopted requirements.

### Evidence discovery

Proposed home: extend [research of unknown references](../contracts/development-discipline.md#unknown-references-are-researched-not-reconstructed)
with the inquiry-specific boundary, and place the detailed procedure in a new
research guide. Preserve [causal analysis](../contracts/development-discipline.md#analysis-exposes-the-decisive-causal-mechanism)
and the existing evidence-chain owner rather than repeating them.

For exploratory, comparative, disputed, or consequential multi-claim inquiry,
identify the important questions and plausible alternatives before committing
to the main explanation. Follow entities, references, data, and disagreements
when they offer a better observation than repeating the original topic query.
Choose relevant breadth for the question; no fixed platform, source, perspective,
or query count establishes coverage. A bounded fact lookup can remain direct.

Keep source observations distinguishable from provisional interpretation and
final claims. Relate decisive evidence to the claim it supports, its origin,
actual reading or measurement scope, and important limitations. Multiple
documents may depend on one observation; independence is assessed for the
claim, not granted by different URLs. A short answer need not create a ledger.

Use provisional explanations to direct inquiry, while testing credible
alternatives and allowing new evidence to change the question itself. Select
further work for its expected contribution to the task: distinguishing
explanations, finding important omissions, or independently reducing uncertainty.
Preserve the strongest unresolved disagreement without manufacturing equal
weight for unsupported positions. No precise information-gain score is required.

Before the main synthesis is delivered, check that its decisive claims fit the
available evidence, that important alternative explanations have been examined
where relevant, and that limitations are visible. Distinguish question resolved,
bounded diminishing returns, access blocked, and budget exhausted. Exhaustion
or repetition within one search path does not establish comprehensive coverage.
Drafting may expose another question and return to discovery.

Select the delivery form for what it adds for the intended reader: a useful
comparison, explanation, decision, or route to an existing source. A concise
annotated reference can be sufficient when rewriting would add no value.
An original narrative is not a mandatory output of successful research.

### Source access and observation limits

Proposed home: extend [capability-preserving fallback](../contracts/development-discipline.md#tool-failure-triggers-capability-preserving-fallback)
with successful-output limitations and reuse, with a separate access guide.
This method also applies when coding or reviewing requires external information;
it is not contingent on launching a research project.

Assess whether the retrieval or measurement method can observe the property
being claimed. Successful extraction does not prove complete page content,
interaction, comments, rendering, or accurate transcription. Missing content
can reflect the method's limits. Keep access failure, incomplete observation,
and evidence of absence distinct. An accessible substitute may support a
narrower claim; its availability does not silently restore the stronger claim.

For costly or recurring access, consult existing permitted method knowledge
before repeating exploration. Retain a concise verified procedure or negative
result where future matching tasks can retrieve it. Identify the source and
capability, relevant environment conditions, observed result and date, limits,
and conditions for revalidation. A method note may be a section of an existing
guide or tool-local store; private account and machine details remain private.
Access-method freshness and source-data freshness are separate questions.

Reconsider further attempts against the question's value and remaining task
budget. A changed tool, agent, or session does not reset spent effort. Further
attempts need a plausible changed condition or useful alternative, rather than
blind repetition. Equally, a previous failure is not a permanent blacklist.
When stopping, preserve the affected claim, remaining access gap, and available
recovery. Reuse is demonstrated only when a later fresh task finds and applies
the record, or correctly invalidates it.

### Routing and responsibility

| Responsibility | Load when | Return to the task owner |
|---|---|---|
| Research method | The question needs exploration, comparison, or consequential evidence assessment | Findings with source relationships, important alternatives, gaps, and the reason for stopping |
| Access method | Source retrieval is uncertain, costly, recurring, or method-sensitive | Material actually obtained, observation limits, and applicable reusable access knowledge |
| Existing Grill | A review is requested or an existing review trigger applies | Important supported challenges to the goal, approach, result, or discipline itself |
| Task owner | Throughout the authorized task | Integrated conclusions, resource choices, corrections, and final delivery |

Modules are reading and responsibility boundaries. A single agent can use them;
delegation follows the existing bounded-assignment rule. The first change would
add compact routes through the existing entrypoint and guides index, not another
startup checklist. It would not install runtime agents, storage, or a scheduler.
If operational experience is currently stored elsewhere, the adopter supplies
a permitted locator instead of copying everything into the public template.

## Why these changes, and how they could fail

Through the leverage-point perspective discussed with the maintainer:

- **Goal:** judge progress by useful understanding and supported delivery, not
  document volume, citations, reviews passed, or quota consumed.
- **Information:** retain source relationships, missing observations, and
  method limitations so later decisions can use them.
- **Feedback:** compare predictions and claims with actual observations and
  owner reactions in their proper domains; test reuse in a later task.
- **Rules:** constrain evidence claims and repeated spending, without making
  every query pass through a fixed research ceremony.
- **Organization:** allow selective context and justified delegation while
  keeping integration and authority explicit.

This mapping is a design interpretation, not a measured ranking of effects.
The principal risks are invented coverage scores, excessive record keeping,
premature stopping, stale access advice, and reviewers repeating shared errors.
Additional ceremony could recreate the problem it is intended to prevent.

## Validation and withdrawal

The following are proposed checks, **not performed behavioral experiments**.
Exercise current and candidate instructions on matched inputs, with comparable
tools and resource envelopes. Retain direct outputs, actual cost, and decisive
observations. Do not grade success by vocabulary, completed forms, or agreement
with the implementer alone. Synthetic cases test instructions, not real-user
benefit; follow with bounded real task use before claiming effectiveness.

| Case | Observation that would support the change | Countercase or failure to inspect |
|---|---|---|
| Several articles repeat one report; a relevant contrary dataset exists | Finds the shared origin, follows a useful new branch, and scopes the conclusion to the resulting evidence | Counts more URLs as corroboration, or keeps searching after a bounded factual question is resolved |
| A consequential claim sits inside a valid, accessible document | Checks the claim's measurement or inference, not only the citation's existence | A long bibliography audit leaves the decisive assertion unexamined |
| A previously difficult source is needed in fresh context | Locates and uses the verified method, or identifies changed conditions and updates it | Repeats the full exploration; treats old failure as permanent; changes tools to restart its budget |
| Extraction succeeds but omits relevant content | Recognizes the observation limit and uses a suitable permitted method or narrows the claim | Reports missing extraction as proof the content does not exist |
| Standards review and fresh-reader use disagree | Preserves both findings and examines the distinct standards, inputs, and artifact | Supplies hidden background to make a confusing explanation pass; invents audience satisfaction or confusion |
| An ordinary direct lookup and a supported conclusion | Completes without unnecessary delegation, forms, or contrived opposition | The method increases effort without changing a supported claim or finding a material omission |

Before any behavioral trial, state its concrete budget and stop condition under
the existing resource rule. One passing task does not establish robustness or
justify a universal gate. A gate or recurring schedule would need its own
evidence and scoped decision.

Recommended implementation order after scope acceptance: reconcile the two
existing inquiry owners; add their on-demand guides and compact routes; inspect
the resulting complete reading path; run proportionate consumer and behavioral
checks; report the verified layer and remaining limits. Do not reopen the whole
autonomy contract in this change. Operational host drift, shared-write control,
and artifact-retention mechanisms remain separate candidates, not an authorized
backlog created by this report.

Narrow or remove a proposed requirement if it repeatedly adds records or review
without discovering material omissions, if normal lookups are burdened, or if
reuse spreads stale evidence. Retain observations and rationale, remove obsolete
routes, and reconcile the owner rather than adding another compensating rule.
