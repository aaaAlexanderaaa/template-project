# Essential reasoning across contexts

## Claim and original scope

The [plan](../plans/2026-10-30-resident-reasoning-and-transfer.md) follows the
owner's request to keep higher-level reasoning available to AI users so they
can recognize missing problems and principles, without assuming recognition
will produce a remedy. Subagent discovery is one example. The implementer's
initial plan and first ChatGPT discussion overfocused on that example; the
owner corrected the scope before normative implementation. The plan was
rewritten around the higher-level outcome rather than treating the example as
the whole request.

This starts from HEAD `3089af1af089aacb4862c9d1dd4ac2991d289fa0` plus the
completed, uncommitted [question/context work](2026-10-30-question-wording-and-context.md).
Its historical trial applies to its recorded snapshots, not automatically to
the subsequently revised guidance.

## Design and supporting evidence

Development now owns a concise, explicitly incomplete set of reasoning cues in
the normal required core. They route to existing judgment, inquiry, prompting,
experience, outcome and transfer rules. The entrypoints explain why the cues
must be available before a specialist problem is recognized. They remain
challengeable, with detailed methods loaded when needed. Known consequential
gaps retain actual disposition and evidence; a rule, assignee or handoff does
not imply implementation, effect or future resolution.

Execution applies the principle to discovery assignments without forbidding
specific verification. Adoption preserves meaning and observes use through the
actual chain claimed. The README no longer describes all failures through a
fixed deficit in executive function; operating conditions are investigated as
possible mechanisms. These are implementation choices within the request,
not an owner-authored final taxonomy of cognition.

Two ChatGPT design discussions were used. The first focused too narrowly on
discovery and transfer. A second, following the owner's clarification, stressed
the difference between knowing, attending and acting, possible failure in the
discipline itself, and observation outside already named categories. Root
integrated those judgments rather than treating peer agreement as acceptance.

Primary-source inspection was limited to:

- [OpenAI, Expanding on what we missed with sycophancy (May 2, 2025)](https://openai.com/index/expanding-on-sycophancy/):
  the report describes how reward signals shape trained behavior and how the
  April update escaped pre-release review. It supports examining the operating
  and evaluation setup, not a diagnosis of this executor's internal motives.
- [Mohamed et al., LLM as a Broken Telephone (ACL 2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.371/):
  the publication page's abstract reports accumulating distortion in iterative
  translation experiments. That narrower setting motivates checking transfer;
  it does not establish loss rates or successful mitigation in this repository.

## Consumer path

A separate ChatGPT learner receives actual source snapshots and a fictional
project's purpose and existing authority. It writes concise local instructions
for another executor without seeing that executor's later cases. A separate
executor receives the returned local text and bounded task data without the
source repository or implementer's explanation. The intermediate text and
first answers will be retained; any recovery or alteration is separate evidence.

This exercises instruction transfer and subsequent decisions, not a real
repository migration or sustained autonomous operation. Separate submitted
conversation IDs can show submitted-history separation only. Configured model,
memory, personalization and complete inherited context remain uninspected.

The [consumer record](2026-10-30-resident-reasoning-consumers.json) preserves the
exact source packet, requests, returned text and root assessment. The learner
produced 481 words of local instructions against an approximate 450-word
context budget. It preserved local authority and the general reasoning rather
than reducing the lesson to subagent discovery. The tool returned an HTML
wrapper; tags were removed and visible entities decoded for the executor.
Both forms are retained. No semantic correction or author explanation was
inserted between learner and executor.

Tool metadata confirmed different submitted conversations for the design,
learner and executor. The executor received only the rendered local instruction
text and fictional task facts. It produced the following decisions:

| Case | Observed use | What remains unestablished |
|---|---|---|
| Passing launch metrics with unexplained operator abandonment | Selected actual operator-task evidence beyond the existing classifier and kept cause and ownership unknown | No UI or task-record probe was executed; no causal diagnosis or real user improvement is claimed. |
| Recorded and assigned status mismatch under a favorable-scorecard incentive | Kept the issue open, preserved backend authority and refused unsupported closure; distinguished documentation from correction | Budget and incentive pressure were facts in a written scenario, not measured runtime pressure. The agent's internal motives are unknown. |
| Recovery investigation framed in advance as copy problems with a five-finding quota | Chose direct read-only investigation, treated jargon as a hypothesis and rejected the quota | Actual subagent launch and an emitted subagent assignment were not tested. Choosing not to delegate was permitted and adds no claim that delegation would be worse. |
| One authorized spelling correction | Chose the direct edit with narrow diff evidence and no new approval or broad review | The exercise forbade writes, so the typo was not actually changed. |
| Further continuation | Preserved proposed versus performed work, open issues, authority and missing evidence | No further executor consumed this continuation. Later generations remain untested. |

Root checked these decisions against the original higher-level request and
local project facts. The result supports the bounded source-to-local-to-decision
path, including use beyond the subagent example. It does not prove that the
guidance caused better performance: there was no control group, comparison of
models, real operation or sustained observation. No verified consumer finding
required a source correction. The decisions remain evidence of instruction use,
not proof that future AI users will reliably apply the principles.
