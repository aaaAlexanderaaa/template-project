following are observations from the preceding investigation, not new runtime

are provenance for a reader with that checkout, never template dependencies.

The study also found substantive differences between pieces using the same
schema: concrete bargaining mechanisms did explanatory work that a theory
label alone did not. This supports retaining shared infrastructure while
letting material determine its use. It does not establish that every common
form is harmful. Human feedback supplied new questions and methods as well
as corrections; reducing it to a prohibition list would lose that knowledge.

The owner's systems framing is consistent with Meadows's attention to
information flows, rules, self-organization, goals and paradigms, including
her cautions about treating the list as a recipe. Here it guides diagnosis of
what a change will amplify; it creates no fixed leverage-point questionnaire
or rule that changing cadence is always low value.
[Primary reference](https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/).

## Existing coverage and concrete changes

The baseline already required positive experience, revisable questions,
bounded active comparisons, calibrated measures, independent direction review,
and method revision. The discovery review and baseline trial below confirmed
that coverage. The implementation therefore concentrates on retrieval,
observation and consumer affordances rather than claiming a missing general
capacity for learning.

| Surface | Before | Change and intended useful behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Task context and operating route | General outcome, inquiry and review rules existed across owners | A conditional diagnosis entry locates the affected choice before prescribing more effort, and distinguishes missing observation from ignored findings |
| Coherent replacement and autonomous method revision | Required useful responsibilities to survive role simplification | Before/after comparison includes actual artifacts, useful distinctions, exploration and improvement duties; the successor exercises their triggers |
| Completion and independent-review templates | Asked for claim, revision, evidence and independence, but not an explicit actual inspection scope | Record the object and sections/cases actually examined, and why that scope can establish the verdict; source and consumer views test different claims |
| Research method and operating example | Correct instructions about letting material change the question, mostly abstract response labels | A fictional garden example shows a species catalog becoming a temporal explanation, with an actual opening, retained value and an explicit catalog countercase |
| Experience owner | Successes and failures were reusable | Constructive owner feedback retains the new question, distinction or method it enables, as well as the error it corrects |

The judgment section routes existing analysis and reuse rather than adding a
new reviewer tier or standing form. It permits decisive locally verified work.
No universal humility language, forced novelty, global memory store, metric,
or mandatory four-quadrant classification is introduced. The source's role
names and elaborate task machinery are not copied.

## Independent discovery and implementation response

A separately dispatched context received a faithful account of the owner's
purposes and could challenge the plan, inspect current owners and propose
enabling improvements. It did not produce or edit this implementation. It
found two defensible opportunities: make the completion template's verdict
fit the actual inspection scope, and demonstrate creative growth through
changed work. It cautioned that most doctrine already existed and that more
general obligations needed behavioral justification.

This was not blind discovery: initial search excerpts exposed the plan, and
the parent later sent a candidate direction. The reviewer preserved its first
judgment and reported that exposure. Parent integration narrowed the judgment
clause to a conditional diagnostic route, prioritized the template affordance,
and added a concrete positive example different from the trial packet.

## Frozen instruction-consumption comparison

The [case packet](2026-10-19-recursive-improvement-cases.json) was frozen before
editing. Six fictional tasks require actual decisions and small artifacts:
heat explanation and work selection; source versus public projection; a method
migration with learning duties; a creative archive introduction; sound parallel
acceleration; and a spelling correction. No private source-project knowledge
or external research is needed. These are intentionally bounded, supplied-fact
tasks rather than a full autonomous direction-selection environment.

Baseline instructions were copied from the stated HEAD. A fresh context
entered through that snapshot's `AGENTS.md`, selected the applicable sections,
and wrote its first answer without another evaluator's verdict. It usefully
completed all six tasks, including the creative rewrite and both direct-work
countercases. It diagnosed the observed-but-dismissed distinction, computed
the unchanged public projection, and preserved nighttime inquiry. This is
positive baseline evidence, not a deficiency to hide.

The revised context also usefully completed all six tasks. The
[trial archive](2026-10-19-recursive-improvement-trials.json) retains the packet
identity, snapshot and instruction hashes, actual dispatches, first response
bodies and context/reading audits. Disposable transport paths are normalized
as declared in that archive; substantive answers are unchanged.

| Case | Baseline artifact or action | Revised artifact or action | Comparative observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: heat explanation and selection | Rejected expansion, reopened the outline exclusion, produced the local 18 C surface / 1 C air comparison and its limits | Rejected expansion, identified observed-but-discounted evidence, changed the release question and produced the same useful distinction | Both diagnose judgment and produce useful work; the revised answer explicitly follows the new distinction, but baseline already made it |
| B: public museum explanation | Rendered identical before/after main text; corrected only the overstated lead | Derived the same unchanged projection and corrected the lead | Both match the claim to the actual boundary; a direct supplied-contract derivation confirms deletion has no main-text effect |

| D: city-route archive | Produced an opening and three-part structure about joke transmission, tested access and naming authority | Produced a coherent opening and structure about making, using and copying maps | Both replace the neglect-only frame, retain the useful human voice and acknowledge the lost simplicity; no real-reader preference was measured |
| E: sound ready work | Four disjoint 75-page assignments and one integrator, with invalidation conditions | Same useful dispatch, retaining valid checks and failure conditions | Both accelerate within authority without a new review program |
| F: spelling | Corrected only the spelling and checked the difference | Same | Direct routine execution remains intact |

The revised reading audit confirms retrieval of the new judgment section
through normal task context. It does not report reading the research guide's
garden example or review templates, so these six cases cannot establish uptake
of those changes. Baseline did read the existing research guide. The audits
therefore preserve different actual reading paths rather than claiming equal
exposure to every method.

Both were separately dispatched with `fork_turns=none`, but shared system and
developer instructions and the injected workspace `AGENTS.md`. They were not
context-free. The model was inherited without an override; its precise runtime
identifier was not independently inspected, and no model variation was tested.
One run per snapshot, sequential dispatch, supplied facts and explicit tasks
cannot isolate an instruction effect or establish autonomous discovery of the
problem. Their useful concrete outputs support compatibility and bounded
consumption. They do not demonstrate an incremental quality gain over baseline.

The direct artifact check retained in the archive derives the public text
before deletion, after deletion and after lead correction, and the temperature
differences. It is a small reproducible derivation from the fictional contracts,
not a deployed system test. No further consumer was commissioned to seek a
more favorable comparison.

## Independent integration review

A fourth separately dispatched context inspected the actual tracked diff,
new records, relevant surrounding authority, current review templates, both
first consumer outputs and their audits. It returned **PASS for bounded
documentation integration**, with no material findings. It confirmed that
both consumers produced useful artifacts, and rejected any inference of
comparative improvement from them. The first judgment was preserved before
reading the newly available trial archive; reading it did not change that
judgment. Its complete report is retained in the same archive.

The review made its actual inspection scope explicit, including uninspected
source-project and runtime evidence. It did not produce this implementation,
but shared the common harness and received the parent's account of owner
purposes, current plan and evidence. This is independent integration judgment,
not independent reconstruction of the original study or a blind audience trial.
It independently checked instruction hashes and `git diff --check`. The
reported test/checker results were not independently rerun by that reviewer.

## Repository validation and closure

Baseline environment: local macOS, Python 3.14.2. The unit suite passed 122
tests in 23.094 seconds, and the normal checker passed with 49 canonical
documents and 16 templates. `docs-policy.toml` declares adopted/full
enforcement. Its checks cover record structure, projections, links and policy,
not creative judgment or operational autonomy.

Checks on the stabilized drafts: the full 122-test suite passed in 23.938
seconds; normal and strict documentation checks passed with 51 canonical
documents and 16 templates; `git diff --check` passed. The suite's fixed clock
advanced from October 10 to October 19 alongside its copied repository fixture;
date validation and production checker behavior were not relaxed. No prose
presence tests or new enforcement machinery were added.

Final public-diff and instruction-identity inspection passed: frozen revised
instruction hashes still match the delivered owners and consumers. The new
public records contain English general methods, portable fictional cases and
normalized transport locators; no personal-memory path, raw private profile,
or disposable absolute path is copied. Information-access and onboarding
projections were reconciled with unchanged behavior; the owner changes add no
new access permission or adoption gate.

Actual bounded use: four subagent dispatches, no correction rechecks, no
external model calls and one public primary-reference read. Billing/token
cost and a sustained performance effect were not measured. No material
finding or human decision remains open for this integration.

Verdict: documentation integration complete in the working tree; bounded
instruction consumption and countercases observed. Baseline success changed
this implementation's scope: fewer added general obligations, clearer normal
entry points and actual worked material. This is a concrete instance of using
comparison to revise the intervention, rather than treating the proposed rule
count as progress.

Existing behavioral promises and dates remain unchanged. This delivery's
bounded observations cannot establish long-term growth, lower correction
cost, or effectiveness for the source project's less capable executor.
